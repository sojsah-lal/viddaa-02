Vidda

Vidda is a personal tool that renders one short satisfying physics animation per day (bouncing balls, marble races) with original music made from public-domain melodies.

The owner logs in with TikTok once. Each day Vidda uploads the new video to the owner's TikTok inbox as a draft. The owner reviews it in the TikTok app, adds a caption, and decides whether to post it. Vidda never posts on its own.

Vidda is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other TikTok users.
